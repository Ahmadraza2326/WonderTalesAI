## 2. The Core Emotional Contract

When a child opens ORBis, the experience must communicate five core feelings:

1. **"I am inside a living world."**  
   The interface is not a SaaS database; it is a celestial realm, a clockwork workshop, a bioluminescent forest, or an ancient library where every button, particle, and sound belongs to the environment.

2. **"My guide knows me and cares about my journey."**  
   Guides (Poly, Newton, Lexi, BEEP-0, Aria, Nova, Sherlock, etc.) are instructional actors. They observe the child's actions, respond with nuanced emotional expressions, celebrate breakthroughs, and change their teaching approach when the child hesitates or struggles.

3. **"I can touch things and make real things happen."**  
   Concepts are not described in abstract text. They are physically manipulated: dragging star gems into rows, weighing alchemical crystals on balance pans, steering robotic execution cursors, and mixing primary pigments.

4. **"When I make a mistake, the world helps me understand."**  
   Mistakes are never met with harsh red buzzers or blunt "Wrong!" prompts. Feedback diagnoses the specific cognitive misconception, gently points attention to the critical detail, and guides discovery step-by-step.

5. **"I want to keep exploring."**  
   Progression feels earned, organic, and wondrous. Completing a lesson unlocks a practice drill that strengthens fluency, which in turn unlocks an ecosystem simulation or 3D flagship game where the learned concept is the core superpower.

## 3. Product Pillars

### Pillar I: Unified World & Continuity
Every activity connects to the broader universe. A story about exploring Mars introduces constellations that the child connects in the *Cosmic Constellation Builder*, uses math concepts taught by *Poly* in the *Citadel of Stars*, and awards stardust used to brew rare creatures in the *Creature Lab*.

### Pillar II: Concept-First Direct Manipulation
Children understand concrete physical models before symbolic abstractions. Notation (like $3 \times 4 = 12$) emerges naturally only *after* the child has grouped, arranged, counted, and experienced the physical array.

### Pillar III: Social & Emotional Pedagogy
Instruction is mediated through warm, conversational, child-directed dialogue and synchronized character acting. The guide maintains eye gaze with interactive targets and demonstrates empathy during struggle.

### Pillar IV: Rigorous Adaptive Mastery
Mastery is multi-dimensional. It evaluates accuracy, hint independence, retention over time, and conceptual transfer in open-ended games. Fake progress and superficial click-throughs are strictly forbidden.

### Pillar V: Uncompromising Aesthetic & Technical Quality
No placeholders, no generic emojis as primary assets, no robotic voices, no unhandled edge cases, and no slow loading times. The standard is internationally competitive with world-class children's media.
