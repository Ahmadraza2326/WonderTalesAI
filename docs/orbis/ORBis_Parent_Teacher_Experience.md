# ORBis Parent & Educator Experience Specification

> **Standard:** Insightful, Trustworthy, Actionable Learning Telemetry  
> **Rule:** Do not expose raw numbers without pedagogical interpretation. Answer: "What did my child learn, what are their superpowers, where do they struggle, and what should we explore together offline?"

---

## 1. The Parent Experience Principles

1. **Clear Learning Translation:** Instead of "Skill 4.2: $80\%$", show: "Aria mastered organizing arrays into multiplication groups today! She quickly solved $3 \times 4$ and $2 \times 6$ without needing hints."
2. **Actionable Off-Screen Prompts:** Provide offline conversation starters: "Next time you bake cookies together, ask your child: 'How many rows of 3 should we make on our baking sheet?'"
3. **Misconception Visibility:** Transparently explain struggle areas: "Leo occasionally confused rows (horizontal) with columns (vertical). Poly gave him a spotlight hint, and he corrected it."
4. **Safety & Screentime Health:** Transparent time tracking with gentle session limits ($15–25\text{ mins}$) and bedtime audio modes.

---

## 2. The Educator & Classroom Specification

* **Classroom Overview:** Real-time student mastery matrix across academic subjects.
* **Misconception Heatmaps:** Highlights which students in a group are experiencing the same conceptual hurdle (e.g. regrouping across zeros or identifying non-rhyming phonemes).
* **Targeted Intervention Dispatch:** Ability to assign specific 5-scene lessons or practice sets directly to individual student profiles.
* **Curricular Standards Alignment:** Direct cross-mapping of ORBis skills to Common Core, NGSS, and international primary frameworks.
