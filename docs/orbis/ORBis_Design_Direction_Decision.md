## 3. Core Visual Principles of the Living Learning Universe

1. **The Universe Container:** The backdrop is a deep, warm celestial night (`#020617` $\to$ `#0f172a`), giving unlimited stage depth without jarring white-screen glare.
2. **Atmospheric Realm Biomes:** Floating islands and cards carry authentic domain textures:
   - **Math:** Luminous celestial geometric constellations and starry grids.
   - **Science:** Living bioluminescent foliage and fluid physics currents.
   - **Reading & Phonics:** Ancient glowing rune parchment and enchanted libraries.
   - **Computer Science:** Clockwork brass gears, neon circuit traces, and robotic registers.
   - **Logic & Creativity:** Prismatic crystal refraction and spectral color dispersion.
3. **Tactile Child-First Affordances:** Primary buttons and manipulatives are 3D-elevated with soft inner glows, clear active press states ($0.97\times$), and haptic feedback.
4. **Zero Emojis as Core Assets:** All character avatars, indicators, and rewards use bespoke SVG vectors, CSS particle systems, and canvas animations.
