## 1. Content Verification Rubric (The 9 Quality Gates)

Every lesson, practice item, book, experiment, and project must pass 9 rigorous quality gates:

1. **Clarity:** Unambiguous instructions with concise, developmental language.
2. **Age Appropriateness:** Content matches cognitive and emotional developmental band (Pre-K to Grade 5).
3. **Pedagogical Value:** Teaches a real, defensible concept aligned with educational standards.
4. **Interactivity:** Provides tactile manipulatives, simulations, or physical cause-and-effect controls rather than passive reading.
5. **Narration Quality:** Conversational, warm, and synchronized audio read-aloud support.
6. **Visual Excellence:** High contrast, cosmic aesthetic, clean hierarchy, and minimum $44\text{px}$ touch targets.
7. **Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA):** High-contrast ratios, focus rings, reduced-motion overrides, and screen-reader semantics.
8. **Feedback Quality:** Instant, encouraging micro-feedback; no punitive shaming on mistakes; progressive 4-tier scaffolding hints without answer leaks.
9. **Replay Value:** Procedural variations, challenge modes, and capstone bridges into flagship games.
